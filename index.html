How to play

Controls
↔️Swipe left / right to change lane. Keys: ← → or A D.
⬆️Swipe up to jump over 🚧. Keys: ↑, W or Space.
⬇️Swipe down to slide under 🛸. Keys: ↓ or S. 🧱 walls need a lane change.
Emoji chats
💬A chat message appears at the top and three emoji icons run down the lanes. Run into the best reaction. Right answers score points, wrong ones break your combo. A tall 🧱 wall may sit right behind an icon: pick that lane and dodge straight after for a BRAVE LANE bonus.
Death spirit and the realm
☠️Now and then a Death Spirit waits at the roadside and swings its stick to push you into a 🌀 portal. Slide in the middle lane to dodge. A wall blocks the far lane.
🌀Get pushed in and you enter the Spirit Realm for 2 minutes: reapers (slide), spear walls and thorns (change lane), nails (jump). Score is doubled and escaping pays a big reward.
💬Chat gates get riskier the further you run: walls sit closer behind the icons.
Friends and mood
💞Each run you chat with a friend. Four messages make a conversation. Good answers build trust and boost your points. Reach 50% trust for a Friendship Bonus.
😄Your mood follows your choices. 😄 JOY gives x1.25 score. 😔 SAD slows the game but pays no bonus.
⚡Emoji powers charge from coins, answers and close calls. Tap 😎 (slow time), 😂 (obstacles become coins) or 😡 (smash walls). Keys 1, 2, 3.
Power-ups
😂Laugh bonus points
❤️Love restores one life
🔥Fire double points for 8 s
😎Cool slows the game for 6 s
⚡Lightning speed boost, smash through obstacles, coins worth double
💀Skull risky bonus chat: big reward, or lose a life
⏸P or Esc pauses. M mutes. Coins unlock new runners in the Emoji Collection.
