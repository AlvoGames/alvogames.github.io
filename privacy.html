Privacy Policy

Emo Chat Runner ("the game"). Last updated: REPLACE_WITH_DATE.

Summary

The game does not ask you to create an account and we do not collect your name, email, location, contacts, photos or any other personal information. Everything the game remembers stays on your device.

What is stored on your device

Your best score, coins, unlocked characters, angels, settings (sound, vibration, difficulty, chat frequency), missions and daily-challenge progress are saved in your device's local storage. We never receive this data. Clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the game deletes it. The in-game "Reset progress" button also deletes it.

Fonts

The game loads its fonts from Google Fonts. When you first open the game online, your device contacts Google's servers to download them, so Google may receive standard connection data such as your IP address. See Google's Privacy Policy. After the first load the fonts are cached and the game works offline.

Purchases

If paid packs are offered, payment is handled entirely by Google Play. We never see or store your payment details. Google Play tells us only that a purchase was completed.

Ads

The game is free and supported by ads: a short ad may appear after every third run, and you can watch optional reward videos for small bonuses. Ads are served by Google AdMob, which may collect and use your advertising ID, IP address and device information to show and measure ads, and to show personalised ads where allowed. See Google's advertising policies. You can reset or opt out of personalised ads in your Android settings (Settings, Google, Ads). A one-time purchase, "Remove Ads", removes the ads shown after runs. (If a release does not include ads yet, say so here before publishing.)

Children

The game is not designed for children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect information from children.
